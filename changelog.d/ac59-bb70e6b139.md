### Fixed

- When a shared extension cell fails to build or start, each member that then runs in its own process shows `packed cell failed:` and the error that ended the shared cell in `/reload --explain`. A retry that succeeded used to hide why the members were not shared. <!-- fix: bb70e6b139 -->
- An extension build that began after its deadline or cancellation no longer fails with `wait for cell build lock: context deadline exceeded` while no lock was held. The error now says the context had ended before the build began. <!-- fix: bb70e6b139 -->
