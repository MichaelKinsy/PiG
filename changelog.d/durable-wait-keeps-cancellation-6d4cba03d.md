### Fixed

- A wait of the durable harness (`Submission.Wait`, `WaitForTask`, `WaitForIdle`) whose context was cancelled before the harness closed, or before its Session failed, now fails with the cancellation, as it does in Pi, not with `ErrClosed` or the Session's failure. Pi rejects a wait at the abort itself (`Waiters.add` runs the abort listener there), so a later `rejectAll` never reaches it and the first rejection wins; PiG's cancellation reached the wait on its own goroutine (`context.AfterFunc`), and a close that came in between gave it the close error. The idle pass also counted a wait whose context was cancelled until that goroutine ran. The wait now listens with `chordctx.OnAbort`: for a context made by `chord/context` it is rejected and out of the set when the cancel returns. A context of another package (an embedder's own `context.WithCancel`, a deadline) still reaches the wait on a goroutine of its own, and the harness reads that context's error where the order matters. The durable library's embedders can see it (stock `pig` does not link `durable/harness`): `git tag --contains` names v0.7.0 as the first tag with the code. <!-- fix: 6d4cba03d -->
