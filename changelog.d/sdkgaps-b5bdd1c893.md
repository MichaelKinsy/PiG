### Added

- The Go, Rust and Python SDKs export Pi's `createExtensionRuntime` and `ExtensionRunner`. The runner is built over the extensions a `DefaultResourceLoader` loaded (they stay in their own processes), dispatches every `emit*` through them in extension order with Pi's chaining and error isolation, answers the registries, and binds the actions, contexts and UI context of the caller. The members of the UI context that take TUI components are served by the TUI handle, not here. The SDK loader takes an additive `runtime` option (D201).
- The same SDKs export Pi's `createAgentSessionRuntime` and `AgentSessionRuntime`: the runtime owns the current session and its services, calls the caller's factory on every `newSession`, `switchSession`, `fork` and `importFromJsonl`, and emits `session_before_switch`, `session_before_fork` and `session_shutdown` to the replaced session's extensions as Pi does. `AgentSession` gains `refreshContext` and `sessionManager`. The `withSession` option is not served yet.
