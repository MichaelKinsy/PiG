### Added

- Go extensions can now use the `AgentSession` members of Pi that a child session from `ctx.CreateAgentSession` lacked: the reads (`IsIdle`, `IsStreaming`, `IsCompacting`, `CurrentSessionID`, `CurrentSessionFile`, `SessionName`, `CurrentModel`, `CurrentThinkingLevel`, `CurrentSystemPrompt`, `SteeringMode`, `GetAllTools`, `GetContextUsage`, `GetSessionStats`, `GetLastAssistantText` and the rest), the changes (`SetThinkingLevel`, `SetScopedModels`, `SetActiveToolsByName`, `SetSteeringMode`, `SetAutoCompactionEnabled`, `SetSessionName`, `ClearQueue`, `ExportToJsonl` and the aborts) and the awaited members, the ones Pi declares `async` (`WaitForIdle`, `SetModel`, `CycleModel`, `Reload`, `Compact`, `NavigateTree`, `ExecuteBash`, `SendUserMessage`, `SendCustomMessage`, `SummarizeForBugReport`, `ExportToHTML`). Each one runs on the host's session over two new calls, `childSession.op` and `childSession.opAsync`, so a value is what the session holds at the call. `pi-agent-core`'s `Agent`, `AgentSession.agent` and the session graph handles of a child (`extensionRunner`, `settingsManager`, `resourceLoader`, `modelRuntime`) stay open and are listed in `test/parity/sdk-exports.toml`.

### Deprecated

- Go: the `SessionID`, `SessionFile`, `Model`, `ThinkingLevel`, `ActiveTools` and `SystemPrompt` fields of `*sdk.AgentSession` still hold what the session had when `ctx.CreateAgentSession` returned, but they are deprecated. Use the methods that read the session's current value: `CurrentSessionID()`, `CurrentSessionFile()`, `CurrentModel()`, `CurrentThinkingLevel()`, `GetActiveToolNames()` and `CurrentSystemPrompt()`, each returning `(value, error)`. The live reads cannot reuse the field names, because a Go field and method cannot share a name, so `SessionID()`, `Model()` and the others do not exist on `*sdk.AgentSession`.
