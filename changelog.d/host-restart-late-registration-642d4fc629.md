### Fixed

- An extension that is restarted automatically after a crash could lose a registration its new process made right after it was ready. The host started the loop that serves the new process's calls before it adopted the new registration into the extension its runner holds, so a command, shortcut, flag or renderer registered late (`registerDeclaration`), a tool registered late (`registerTool`), an event handler subscribed late (`event.subscribe`) or the claim to own thinking runs (`ownThinkingRuns`) was written into the held extension and then replaced by the registration the new process had sent before its ready. The call was answered with success and the registration was not served: the command did not run, the tool or the handler was never called, for as long as that process ran. The restart now adopts the registration before the loop starts, so every call the new process makes after its ready is applied on top of it. The first load of an extension is not changed. No report of this was received; a test holds the adoption and shows each of the four calls lost.
