### Changed

- Durable storage on SQLite does less Go work per read and per commit. The native connection is driven through modernc.org/sqlite's own driver connection instead of `database/sql` (one dedicated connection needs no pool, argument conversion or row scanning), a record read decodes the row's text where it is read instead of building a map per row and copying the text, an entry record decodes through its own single-pass decoder without the generic decoder's validation scan first, and the serial operation queue allocates nothing when no operation waits. On the storage benchmark's SQLite reads (n=12, same host, pinned) the geometric mean is 28% faster (an exact entry lookup 40%, an entry page scan 56%, the fork-depth head lookup 31%, a document replay 23 to 27%), with 29% fewer allocations and 43% fewer bytes per read; a commit allocates 11 to 17% less and its time is unchanged within the noise of the host. Statements, pragmas, stored rows and every query result are unchanged.
