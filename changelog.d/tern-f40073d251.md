### Changed

- A TypeScript extension's session starts with a smaller heap. The extension host's model catalog no longer decodes every built-in model's record into ordered members to learn that the model is unchanged: encoding the 1,609 built-in models allocates 13.1 MB instead of 41.1 MB and keeps 1.2 MB instead of 4.0 MB, and the process holds about 10 MB less at ready (PSS of PiG plus Node with one TypeScript extension, linux/arm64: 173.6 to 163.5 MB with a warm cache and 204.6 to 194.8 MB with a fresh one, against 142.0 MB for Pi with the same extension). The model objects an extension reads are unchanged.
