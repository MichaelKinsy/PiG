### Added

- `bench/k8s` runs PiG's Durable benchmarks on Kubernetes. `go run ./bench/k8s/cmd/k8sbench` submits one Guaranteed-QoS Job per session, runs PiG and pi-durable interleaved in the same pod on the same pinned cores, records the node's kernel, CPU, cpuset, CFS quota and throttling, and writes durable-report's tracker format. `calibrate` measures the reference's noise floor, and `compare` refuses to report when it exceeds `-max-cv`. `bench/k8s/image` builds the pinned benchmark image. Bundle mode needs no registry: the pod runs the pinned official Node image, and the runner copies a prebuilt artifact bundle (`k8sbench bundle`) into it with `kubectl cp`. The pod checks the bundle's and each artifact's sha256 before it runs. On shared cores the noise gate judges CPU time (the cgroup's usage and each turn's process CPU) and the deterministic store size, and reports wall times as informational (`-gate auto|wall|cpu`). A calibration judges a size only from 12 runs. `-pick-node` runs on the node with the most free CPU (allocatable minus requests), and `-spread N` or `-nodes` run one Job per node at once and report the median over them. With `-nodes`, `-spread` and `-pick-node` choose among the named candidates. Without permission to list pods in all namespaces or to list nodes, picking falls back to the namespace's own pods and the candidates. A profile may have several pig targets; each writes its own tracker line per size under its own rule. A session refuses measured cores whose cpufreq governor scales the clock (`-allow-frequency-scaling` runs it unjudged, and such a calibration cannot vouch for a comparison), and records the cores' clock per process. See [Benchmarks on Kubernetes](docs/site/docs/benchmarking.md).

### Changed

- `durable/interop/bench.mjs` reports the pi-durable version that resolved, not a fixed `1.0.4`. It and `bench/durableperf` report the process CPU time of the open and of each measured turn (`openCpu`, `turnCpu`).
