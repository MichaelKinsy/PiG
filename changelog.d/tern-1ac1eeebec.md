### Fixed

- RPC mode now writes the members of an `extension_ui_request` that Pi writes empty, as Pi's `JSON.stringify` does: `message: ""` of `notify`, `title: ""` of `setTitle`, `text: ""` of `set_editor_text` (how a client clears its editor), `statusText: ""` of `setStatus` and an empty `options` list of `select` were dropped, so a client could not tell empty from absent. `input` and `editor` no longer write `placeholder: ""` / `prefill: ""` when the extension passed none. In the Go, Python and Rust SDKs `SetStatus(key, "")` is now an empty status text (it used to remove the entry), and `ClearStatus` / `clear_status` is Pi's `setStatus(key, undefined)`; an empty placeholder or prefill is the unset member (Python: `None`). The interactive footer is unchanged. <!-- fix: 1ac1eeebec -->
