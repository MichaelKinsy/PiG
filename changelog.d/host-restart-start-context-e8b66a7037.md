### Fixed

- An extension that is restarted automatically after a crash could be judged stopped by the host, or not, from a half-replaced record. The restart rewrote the context its new process runs under and the owner context whose end means the host stopped the extension, while a failed command, shortcut or event call read the owner context from its own goroutine; the race detector reported the pair once in a gate run. The two now change together under the lock that already guards the process handle, and every reader copies them under it.
