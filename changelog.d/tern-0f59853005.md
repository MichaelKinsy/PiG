### Fixed

- RPC mode no longer writes `entry_appended` for a `pi.appendEntry` or `session_info_changed` for a `pi.setSessionName` that an extension's `session_start` handler makes, and `--mode json` no longer writes anything a `session_start` handler produced: its `entry_appended`, `session_info_changed`, and the `message_start` and `message_end` of a `sendMessage`. Pi subscribes its stdout writer after the extensions are bound, so none of these reach a client; the JSON stream now starts at the prompt, after the session header. The `session_start` of a replacement command (`new_session`, `switch_session`, `fork`) that runs after the writer exists is still written, as in Pi. <!-- fix: 0f59853005 -->
