### Fixed

- A submission's `Wait` no longer fails with "Harness is closed" when a close begins before the wait wakes: a host `SubscribeCommits` listener that closes the Harness on the commit that settled the submission, or a close that comes between that commit's adoption and its publication. Pi resolves the wait at the commit, so a later close cannot reach it; PiG settled it only after the host listeners, and the close's seal rejected it, turning a settled submission into an error for its waiter. The record is now fixed at the commit and the wait only wakes after the listeners. <!-- fix: 6c26be37c26a26540b42f19d4d1df597b5cf419f -->
- `WaitForIdle` on the Harness or a Conversation no longer fails with "Harness is closed" when a close begins after the commit that made the work idle and before the wait wakes. Pi settles the idle waits at the commit, so a later close cannot reach them; PiG decided and settled them only after the host listeners, and the close's seal rejected them. They are now settled at the commit and only wake after the listeners. <!-- fix: 11866f8f178657151179a0f39e1a7b03864016e2 -->
