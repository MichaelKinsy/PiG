### Added

- Chord's host traces its work when it has a telemetry adapter (`HostOptions.Telemetry`; Pi main `c23b9ec1`): `chord.load`, `chord.reload` and `chord.revoke` spans and a root's `trace` option, a consumer's `chord.hold` and `chord.call`, a provider's `chord.method` (a type served untraced starts none), `chord.activate` with its `chord.park` and `chord.retry` events, `chord.resolve`, `chord.routine`, a job's `chord.job` intent, run and finish, native open and close, `ctx.trace`, the relay's `chord.relay` for relayed holds, re-holds and calls, and `chord.http` for each routed request (`HttpOptions.Telemetry`, its method and route pattern, never its path; a `traceparent` continues only when a contributed relay verified the request). A hold and a call carry their trace on the link (`HoldMessage.Trace`, `CallMessage.Trace`: at most 8 keys, each name and value at most 256 printable ASCII characters, kept in the order sent), so a provider's spans continue its consumer's trace and a re-hold links to the trace it continues. `HostOptions.PrincipalAttributes` says who a span was for (`chord.principal`, capped). New metrics: `chord.link.bytes`, `chord.links.open`, `chord.holds.active`, `chord.calls.in_flight`, `chord.link.state_messages`, `chord.backpressure.wait` and `chord.park.duration`. A host with telemetry off, or an untraced adapter, starts no span and records nothing; a document's commit allocates nothing more for it.
