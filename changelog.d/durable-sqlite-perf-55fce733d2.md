### Changed

- The native SQLite engine is now modernc.org/sqlite v1.62.0 (SQLite 3.54.1, with modernc.org/libc v1.80.1 and memory v1.12.1). A Durable commit on SQLite storage is 28 to 41% faster (one entry 112 to 66 us, 100 entries 3.49 to 2.50 ms, a mixed entry 425 to 299 us, warm; a fresh store's first commit 22 to 26% faster) and opening a store is 31% faster; reads are unchanged or faster (the filtered task scan 18%, an exact document address 7%). Allocation counts are equal and bytes per operation grow by at most 3%. Statements, pragmas and rows are unchanged.
- A prepared SQLite statement keeps one compiled copy per pattern of 0 and 1 parameters it is run with. SQLite 3.54 plans a statement again whenever a bound integer moves between 0 or 1 and any other value, and Durable's fork-depth head lookup binds the root conversation (id 1) at the end of every walk and a deeper one at the start of the next, which made that read 18% slower on the new engine; it is now 7% faster than before the upgrade.
