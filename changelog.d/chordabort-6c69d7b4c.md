### Fixed

- Chord: a wait on a promise that a context's abort also ends (`awaitWithContext` in Pi) now delivers the abort in the section that made it, ordered with the promise's own settlement, as Pi does (the abort listener runs inside `abort()`, the settlement's continuation is a microtask). Before, the abort was delivered by `context.AfterFunc` on a goroutine of its own and could lose to a settlement that came a moment after it. The visible case: an attempt sealed while it waited for the previous attempt's drain (a decision change, or a reload, that sealed the successor as the old drain ended) could still run its factory once the drain ended, so a plugin saw the factory of an attempt that had been abandoned run (`[a:write a:write]` where Pi has `[a:write]`). `chord/context` gains `OnAbort`, the listener that runs inside the cancel function, in the cancelling section; an abort made from another goroutine is queued on the host's loop as an event when it is observed. <!-- fix: 6c69d7b4c38aeac73f5a993e82ff856cce23863c -->
