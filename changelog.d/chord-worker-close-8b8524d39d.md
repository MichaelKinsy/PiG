### Fixed

- chord: closing the workers component waits for the goroutines that read the streams of the workers that exited. A process outside a worker's tree that still held its output made the server keep the stream's reader after the worker's exit; Close released the stream without waiting for that reader, so a reader inside the app's OnOutput (or in a listener of the link) went on running and told what it had read to the app after Close returned, and its goroutine and descriptor outlived the component. Close now waits for those readers, bounded by its context, and nothing of a released worker's streams is told once it returned.
