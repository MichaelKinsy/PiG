### Fixed

- Chord: `chordctx.OnAbort` on a context derived by `WithAbortSignal` from a signal made with `WithCancel(WithoutAbortSignal(x))` and from an independent source now hears the reason of the source that aborted first. Before, the order that picks it (`chordctx.AbortOrder`) walked through the `WithoutAbortSignal` context into its parents, so the abort of `x`, which never reached the derived context, counted as that context's own and its cause won over the independent source's although that one aborted first (a listener added after the aborts heard `x`'s reason). As in Pi, where `withoutAbortSignal` leaves the context no abort signal, the walk now ends at a `WithoutAbortSignal` context; the chord host's linked abort signals rank their two sources by the same function. Released in: no release (the `WithoutAbortSignal` marker that the walk ignored came with 32168e142, which no tag contains; v0.7.0 does not have it). <!-- fix: 873f87dc7 --> <!-- cause: abortSources and AbortOrder each walked a context's parents on their own, and only abortSources stopped at a WithoutAbortSignal context, so the two functions disagreed about which aborts reach a context. -->
