### Fixed

- Selecting an OpenRouter routing variant (`openrouter/<model>:nitro` or `:floor`) at startup now uses the window, limits and prices of your personalized OpenRouter list, as the divergence record D199 says a variant does ("API, base URL, prices, limits ... are the base model's"); the variant used to be built from the bundled catalog, with its stale window and prices, or from the provider's default model when the bundled catalog lacks the base. The routing variants are a PiG addition (D199), so this is a fault in that addition and not a difference from Pi.
