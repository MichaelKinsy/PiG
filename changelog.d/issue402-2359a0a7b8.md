### Fixed

- In macOS Terminal.app, which sends Option+Up as plain Up in its default profile, plain Up with an empty editor and a queued message now restores the queued messages to the editor and clears the queue, as Option+Up does in other terminals; before, Up recalled history while the message stayed queued and was still sent after the tool, even after you deleted the recalled copy. The queued-message hint names Up in that state. Elsewhere (tmux, ssh, other terminals), when Up recalls history while a message is queued, PiG shows one dim line under the editor, once per run, saying the text is a copy and which key edits the queue; tmux, ssh and unknown terminals also get the Terminal.app mapping of Option+Up to `\033[1;3A`. Pi 1.5.0 behaves as PiG did before (D228). <!-- fix: 2359a0a7b8 -->
