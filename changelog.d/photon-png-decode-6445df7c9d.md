### Fixed

- PNG images are now decoded the way Pi decodes them, pixel for pixel and error for error on every file of the oracle corpus, where PiG used to use Go's `image/png`. A PNG is read as Photon's png crate reads it: every colour type and bit depth with its transparency chunk (a grayscale or RGB tRNS chunk longer than its colour, one shorter than the palette), Adam7 interlacing, 16-bit samples converted the way Photon converts them, the first image of an animated PNG, ancillary chunks with a wrong checksum skipped and critical ones refused, a file that stops right after its image data, the memory limit of the crate, and a zlib stream read by its own inflater (which refuses and accepts other streams than zlib's). A clipboard image, an attachment, a tool-result image, an ICO entry and a file handed to the terminal image conversion or to the durable `read` tool are converted or resized as Pi converts or resizes them, and a damaged PNG is accepted or refused as Pi accepts or refuses it. PiG no longer registers Go's PNG decoder for its own use. <!-- fix: 6445df7c9d -->
