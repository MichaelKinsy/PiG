### Changed

- Chord's Go HTTP and workers API takes Pi's shapes where it differed: `Authenticator` and `WorkerPlugins.Load` take the context first, `NodeRequest` is one struct tagged by `Kind` (`NodeRequestRequest` or `NodeRequestRefused`) with a closed `WireStatus`, `SameSite` is the string union `"Strict" | "Lax"`, a contribution's sockets are `HttpSocketEntry{Pattern, Socket HttpSocketOptions}` (`SocketFunc` makes the plain one), `Router.Hosts` and `Router.Framers` are `[]string`, `Workers.State` and `WorkerResult.Busy` are `chord.Watchable[delta.JsonValue]` (`host.Expose` makes one of a reference's member), `Workers.Inputs` is the file list of the worker base, `WorkerLoad.Core` is the app's Go facets (Pi's `core`) and `NodeCore` the JavaScript ones, and `telemetryingest.Batch` is `TelemetryBatch`.
