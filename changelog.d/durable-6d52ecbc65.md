### Changed

- The Durable core follows pi-durable 1.4's `Storage.commit(writes, adopt, context)`: a Storage adopts a durable batch by calling `adopt(seq)` once, synchronously, before it resolves, and the core applies and publishes the commit inside that call. `subscribeCommits()` listeners run one microtask after the adoption, and a task run is signalled to abort after them. A commit the Storage rejects after adopting it stays published and counted, and then fails the Session; a call that rejects before adopting discards what was staged, and one that resolves without adopting fails the Session. Observers of the Session that throw fail it once the Storage returned. The core's own storage adapters (memory, SQLite, JSONL-over-SQLite) and the storage conformance provider adopt the same way.
