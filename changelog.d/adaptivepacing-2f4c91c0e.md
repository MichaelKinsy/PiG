### Added
- `terminal.framePacing: "adaptive"` in the global settings file, or `PIG_FRAME_PACING=adaptive` for one run (off unless set), lets fullscreen mode begin the next frame of a streaming reply 10 to 16 ms after the last one began: PiG waits four times what the last frame took to draw and write, at least 10 ms and at most Pi's 16 ms. What a frame shows is unchanged, and a key press and the last frame of a reply are never held back. It applies only on terminals known to show a frame at once at its end (synchronized updates): kitty, Ghostty, WezTerm, Warp, iTerm2, Alacritty, and the VS Code terminal from 1.108 on, never inside tmux, GNU screen, Zellij or herdr, and never while an extension draws on the screen. By default, and with `"pi"`, frames begin 16 ms apart, as Pi's do. Measured with the terminal declared as Alacritty, the median time between visible changes falls from 15.6 to 9.9 ms, but while a small file is written about a third of the changes come 20 ms apart where Pi's come 16 ms apart, and PiG uses 5 to 8 % more CPU time (D625).
