### Fixed

- `tool_search` and codemode `searchTools()` (the coding agent's and durable's) find tools by words in any script, not only ASCII letters and digits. The text is NFC-normalized and split into words with Unicode word segmentation (the ICU dictionaries split Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Lao, Khmer and Myanmar text without spaces, such as `搜索记录`, into words), so `café` and `cafe\u0301` match and `Поиск документов`, `البحث عن الملفات` and `फ़ाइल खोजें` are searchable. This replaces PiG's earlier one-term-per-character matching of Chinese, Japanese and Korean (D173), and a Piglet Binary built without the word dictionaries (D92) keeps such a run as one term.
