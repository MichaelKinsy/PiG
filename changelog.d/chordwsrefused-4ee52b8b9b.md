### Fixed

- A host whose parent link had closed before `Connect` registered it (a server that refused the WebSocket at once, a proxy that reset it) no longer keeps the dead link as its parent: its references wait for the next link, a hold with no other route fails at once with `no route to server`, and the next good `Connect` holds the references again. The host used to keep the dead link, bind every hold to it and wait on it, and the next `Connect` left a hold that was already set where it was. A WebSocket link made over a socket that had closed already is a closed link too; it used to stay open over the closed socket. <!-- fix: 4ee52b8b9b --> <!-- cause: a Go link closes on its own goroutine, between any two statements of its creator, where Pi's connect() runs to completion before any socket event: a link closed before the host registered it ran its close handler before the connection was the host's upstream, so the handler had nothing to clear and Connect then kept the dead link as the parent; likewise a WebSocket link read nothing of a socket that had closed before its close listener existed, so it stayed open over the closed socket -->
