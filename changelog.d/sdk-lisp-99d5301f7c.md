### Added

- Common Lisp extensions. `pig extension init --lang lisp` scaffolds an ASDF system whose `new-extension` factory registers tools, commands, shortcuts, flags and event handlers through the `pig` package of the new Lisp SDK, and Pig builds it into a cached, uncompressed SBCL 2.6.9 image (about 9 ms to start) that it runs isolated or packed beside other Lisp extensions. Lisp extensions may depend on Quicklisp libraries: a system listed in `:depends-on` is resolved from the pinned Quicklisp dist 2026-01-01 with its dependencies, the dist's index files checked against recorded SHA-256 digests and every archive against its listed size and MD5 and a recorded SHA-256; anything else fails the build, and `PIG_OFFLINE` builds from the cache only. The SDK covers the exports listed as native in `docs/extension-sdk-exports.md`; the rest are named gaps of the `SdkLispPhase2` lane. SBCL runs on Linux, macOS and Windows (Winsock AF_UNIX), and the Windows CI job runs the Lisp conformance rows.
