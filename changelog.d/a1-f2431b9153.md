### Fixed

- The session selector, the `/llama` search and the `/mcp` menus move their selection on `tui.select.pageUp` and `tui.select.pageDown`, and a page key bound to Ctrl+V pages an open autocomplete menu instead of pasting an image. In fullscreen mode a focused list that handles those keys receives them instead of the transcript. <!-- fix: f2431b9153 -->

### Changed

- The session selector no longer keeps its selection valid when a page key arrives before any session has loaded; like Pi, it stays unselected until a key moves it.
