### Fixed

- Lower- and upper-casing in `tui`, `internal/codingagent` and `internal/nodespawn` keeps a lone UTF-16 surrogate unchanged, as JavaScript's `toLowerCase` and `toUpperCase` do. `"a\uD800b"` stays `"a\uD800b"` instead of gaining three U+FFFD, so two environment names that differ only in a lone surrogate are no longer merged by the Windows environment-name dedupe, and a Σ beside a lone surrogate is lowered as beside any other non-letter.
- A glob filter, model scope or other `internal/minimatch` pattern with a numeric brace range beyond 2^53 expands as Pi's brace-expansion 5.0.12 does, in JavaScript number arithmetic: `x{99999999999999999999..99999999999999999999}` matches `x100000000000000000000` and no longer `x57`, and `x{9007199254740993..9007199254740995}` matches `x9007199254740992`.
