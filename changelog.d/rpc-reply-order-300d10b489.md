### Fixed

- An RPC client could receive a Node extension's notification or other fire-and-forget UI call (`notify`, `setStatus`, `setWidget`, `setTitle`, `setEditorText`, `pasteToEditor`, or `entry_appended` from `pi.appendEntry`) before the reply of the command that caused it (`set_session_name`, `set_thinking_level`, `cycle_thinking_level`, or `set_model` / `cycle_model` when the thinking level changes without an awaiting `model_select` handler).
