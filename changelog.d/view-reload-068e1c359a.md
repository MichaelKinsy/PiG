### Changed

- A `/reload`, and `ctx.reload()` in every mode, now ends the view mounts (`ctx.ui.mountView` and its forms in the other SDKs) of the extensions it reloads: every attached view client, the terminal included, is sent each such mount once more in state `broken` with reason `owner_gone`, and the reloaded extension mounts its keys again under a new epoch. Before, the mount of the instance before the reload stayed live and drawn, and the reloaded extension's mount of the same key failed with `invalid` ("already mounted"). An extension that mounts when it starts or when a client arrives needs no change; one that took its old mount over through `viewState(key)` now finds it `broken` and mounts again. An intent raised on the ended mount is refused. A client of the view endpoint keeps its connection and its attachment across the reload, as before.
