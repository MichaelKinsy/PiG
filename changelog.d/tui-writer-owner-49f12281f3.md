### Fixed

- tui: a renderer that draws on its own frame timer (one with no render dispatcher: the `tui.alt` screen of an extension, a program that embeds the package) wrote a frame from the timer goroutine while `ShowOverlay`, `HideCursor`, `ShowCursor`, `WriteRaw` or a stop wrote from the caller: two goroutines were in the terminal writer at once, and a cursor or raw sequence could land among the bytes of a frame, between its rows and its cursor placement. Every write of a renderer now holds its writer lock and a frame keeps it from its first write to its last, so such a write is before the frame or after it, as on the one event loop of Pi; a write a component makes from its `Render` still goes before the bytes of its frame. The interactive mode of pig draws and calls from one loop and showed no fault. <!-- fix: 49f12281f39f2c52e70bea48f1a3f7d09569b725 -->
