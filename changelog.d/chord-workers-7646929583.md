### Added

- Go API `chord/nextnode/workers` (Pi `next-node/workers` at `6d4b7443`): Chord v2's workers component. `CreateWorkers` routes a host kind to one worker process per address (started on demand, stopped when idle, never restarted by itself: a crash, a `fatal`, a lost link or a failed start leaves the address failed until `Retry` or a rollout), `WorkerMain` is the worker's side (start message, process lock, plugin runtime over the shipped scripts, `ready` and idle flips, reloads, shutdown), and `ProcessLock` is the kernel lock of one live worker per address. A worker is a child process in its own process group (killed with everything it spawned) with an IPC channel beside its stdout and stderr. <!-- feat: chord-nextnode-workers -->
