### Changed

- A keystroke in the editor allocates less per frame, and so does a streamed Markdown token. Measured at go1.27.4 with the interactive mode's frame wiring, a keystroke cost 15.3 allocations and 1,021 bytes and now costs 7.2 and 712 (the same under 10 and under 1,000 history messages), and a streamed token cost 36.0 allocations and 3,669 bytes and now costs 30.3 and 3,407 (the same under 10 and under 1,000 history messages, and 0.5 fewer per 100 tokens already in the message). The frame is written through one buffer sized for it, the cursor-column escape is built once per column, the row of the cursor is resolved and stripped in one pass, a short line the editor re-wraps reuses its cached chunk, and a Markdown row is padded in two allocations instead of three. What the terminal receives is unchanged.
