### Fixed

- Images in the formats Photon decodes beyond PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP and TIFF are now decoded the way Pi decodes them: ICO (a bitmap or PNG entry, with its AND mask), PNM (PBM, PGM and PPM in plain and raw form, and PAM), DDS (DXT1, DXT3 and DXT5, also through the DX10 header), Radiance HDR and farbfeld. A clipboard target such as `image/x-icon`, an image the model is sent under any declared type, an attachment, and a file handed to the terminal image conversion or to the durable `read` tool are converted to PNG or resized exactly as Pi does, byte for byte, where PiG used to say it could not convert them. Files Photon refuses are refused, an image of more than 512 MiB decoded is refused, and an image with no pixels is no longer encoded (Pi's PNG encoder throws on it). TGA, AVIF, OpenEXR and QOI stay unsupported, as in Pi (TGA has no signature, the others are not built into Photon). <!-- fix: bba3679802 -->
