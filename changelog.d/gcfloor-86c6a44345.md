### Changed

- An interactive run keeps a heap goal of at least 16 MiB (D621): after each collection it collects next at the larger of 16 MiB and the goal of its GC percent, which stays 150 until more than 8 MiB of heap is live and is 100 from then on, also when the heap has shrunk again. On the fake-model benchmark the interactive scenario's CPU falls about 7 % and its time to interactive 12 %, a session of five prompts runs 14.5 % fewer user instructions, and a long session 1.6 to 3.2 % fewer user instructions per prompt; the interactive scenario's peak own memory rises from 16.8 to 20.2 MB and an idle session keeps about 1.1 MB more Go memory. `GOGC` or `GOMEMLIMIT` in the environment turn the policy off as before. <!-- change: 86c6a44345 -->
