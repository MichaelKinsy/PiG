### Changed

- CI: the approval of a divergence or additive record binds a hash of the record's normative text. The `SCRUTINIZED:approved` line carries `text-sha256:<16 hex>` (and a date once the owner approves), and `make divergence-quality`, part of `make check` and of the bookkeeping CI group, fails when the text of an approved record changes without a new approval line, naming the record, the bound hash and the current hash. Evidence blocks (tests, scenarios, call-site markers), inline repository paths and test names, link targets, Pi pin versions, whitespace and wrapping are not in the hash. Only the owner approves a changed text (`make divergence-approve ID=D<N>`); a contributor lists the record in `automation/ci/divergence-reapproval-pending.txt` with a class and a reason, which the gate prints on every run. 80 records are bound to their current text; the 82 whose text changed after their bare, copied marker was first written stay listed until the owner approves them again.
