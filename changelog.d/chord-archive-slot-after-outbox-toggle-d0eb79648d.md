### Fixed

- Chord sync: a store whose outbox was turned off and on again after it committed could not upload any more (`UploadSession` failed with `OutboxUnavailableError` on every attempt) because the commits between what the remote held and the outbox start were gone. `UploadSession` now creates an archive slot after the remote head (the store `Export`), so the session history continues from a full copy; `RemoteHistoryOf` and `PullSession` restore from the newest archive slot along the chain (copies behind it get `HistoryUnavailableError` and restore fresh), and `FoldSession` folds every unfolded slot covered by the snapshot including those before an archive slot (follows Pi `f472efdc1`). <!-- fix: d0eb79648d --> <!-- cause: UploadSession only exported an archive slot when synced == LogStart && outbox.Floor() > 0, so toggling the outbox off and on after a prior upload left outbox.Floor() > synced > 0 and failed with OutboxUnavailableError on every retry. -->
- Chord sync: a `PullSession` restore whose `store.ImportStore` failed before reading the archive body (such as on a cancelled context or non-empty backend) left the remote body file open, and concurrent closes of a `FileSystemSyncRemote` body raced on a plain bool. `PullSession` now closes the body on the `ImportStore` error return and `fileBody.Close` is once-guarded (follows Pi `434dc5e4d`). <!-- fix: 3b0319bcde --> <!-- cause: PullSession omitted body.Close() on the ImportStore error return and fileBody.closed was an unguarded bool under concurrent background close. -->
- Chord store/sync: cancelling a store import while `DecodeJsonl.Next` was in flight raced with `importEach` closing the part source in a background goroutine on `jsonlDecoder.finished` (and `gunzipBody.done`/`reader`). `jsonlDecoder.finished` and `gunzipBody.done` now use `atomic.Bool`, and `gunzipBody.Close` closes its source once with `sync.Once`. <!-- fix: f0f349cb03 --> <!-- cause: importEach calls parts.Close() in a background goroutine on early exit while parts.Next(ctx) may still be running, but jsonlDecoder.finished and gunzipBody.done/reader were unguarded against concurrent Close. -->
