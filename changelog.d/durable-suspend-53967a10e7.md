### Added

- Go: the Durable runtime's suspendable work (Pi `473515f9c`). Signals: `durable.DefineSignal` defines a typed JSON value delivered to a live task from outside with `harness.Signal(ctx, harness, taskId, token, key, value)` or `durable.Signal(tx, ...)`, which answer `SignalDelivered`, `SignalDuplicate`, `SignalInvalid`, `SignalClosed`, or `SignalNotFound`; a value is kept in the task, also one that arrives before the sleep on its key, which then wakes at once; `durable.ReadSignal` on `TaskRuntime`, `HookApi`, and `ToolExecutionApi` reads it, migrated to the token's version, until the task ends. A sleep may wait for signals (`TaskState.Signals`, `Until` optional) and ends at whichever comes first, and a wait may use `JoinAny`; a woken task reads why in `TaskState.Wake` (`WakeTime`, `WakeExpired`, `WakeSignal`, `WakeTasks`). `Harness.Wake()` reports parked work inactive.
- Go: tools that park. `Execute` may return a `ToolExecutionResult` whose `Park` holds a `ToolPark` (`Sleep` or `Wait`) and runs again, once woken, with `api.Wake()` and `api.Checkpoint()` set; `ToolRegistration.Cancel` cleans up the external work of a parked call that is aborted; an invalid park ends the call with an `invalid_park` result, and wrappers never park. `ExecuteToolNoWait` starts or finds a nested call and returns a `NestedToolCall`, `CreateTaskErased` takes a `ToolTaskOptions.Key`, explicit keys are required after a wake, and a restart keeps the `AbandonOnRestart` children of a call that was parked or not started. `SubmissionDraft.Notify` delivers `durable.SubmissionSettledSignal` (Pi's `SubmissionSettled`) to a task when the submission settles.
- Go: `BeforeToolResult.Sleep` parks a call at its gates; every wake, registry change, and open runs the whole `call` phase again. `HookApi.Now()` reads the Harness clock. `afterTools` and `onYield` hooks may ask for tasks (`HookTasks`, `YieldTasks`) that run as the generation's children: an `afterTools` task ends the run with `AfterToolsDecision{Terminate: true}`, and `onYield` tasks decide with `YieldDecision` whether the answer stands while the run's inputs stay open (`LiveRun.Yield`). Kept contexts expire while a conversation's live work is all parked.

### Changed

- Go: `harness.RunEndTask`, `RunEndTasks`, and `RunEndTaskOf` are `HookTask`, `HookTasks`, and `HookTaskOf`; `GenerationHooks.AfterTools` returns `(*HookTasks, error)` and `OnYield` returns a `YieldResult` (`*YieldContinue` or `*YieldTasks`). `ToolExecutionApi.CreateTaskErased` and `durable.ToolCreateTask` take `ToolTaskOptions`. `TaskState.Until`, `TaskInspectionState.Until`, and `TaskGraphState.Until` are optional. `durationMs` of a tool result is the span from the tool task's first run (`StartedAt`) to its result on the Harness clock, parks, downtime, and reruns included; a record written before `StartedAt` existed keeps the old measure. `pi.tool` is version 4 and `pi.generation` version 3: a database written by this version is not meant to be opened by an older Harness. The Durable core backend (`durable/core`) stays at its contract pin (Pi 1.4.0+e7a0df27) and refuses the new members with `driver.ErrSuspendableWorkNotInCorePin`.
