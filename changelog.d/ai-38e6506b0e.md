### Fixed

- The faux provider (`ai.NewFauxProvider`) no longer ends a streamed chunk inside a multibyte character. It cut a response at byte offsets where Pi slices a JavaScript string, so a text delta of non-ASCII text could end halfway through a character, and the partial arguments of a tool call that such a chunk extended were not valid text: every one of those chunks copied and checked all the arguments again. A tool call of 180 KB of mixed-width text allocated 3.2 GB and took 12.7 s (74 s under `-race`) before it ran; it now allocates 25 MB and takes 48 ms. A chunk is `tokenSize * 4` UTF-16 units, as in Pi's `splitStringByTokenSize`, and ends at the first character boundary at or after that size. <!-- fix: 38e6506b0e, b119c53026, 6b64bc1bee -->
