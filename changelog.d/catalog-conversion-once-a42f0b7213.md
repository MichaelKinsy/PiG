### Changed

- A metadata refresh of the model registry converts the bundled model catalog once where it converted it three times. The composer's model list, its all-models read and the provider model data of a registration each ran every listed model through the catalog conversion; they now share one converted snapshot per provider, built on first read and replaced as a whole when the stripped APIs change, so a reader that began before a refresh keeps the models of the snapshot it loaded and never sees a mix of two. Pi reads the runtime's snapshot in process in the same way. BenchmarkSessionRegistryMetadataRefresh, one processor, 200 iterations, three rounds each, on a loaded 192-processor host (load average 80 to 130): 928 allocations and 138.9 KB per refresh before, 233 allocations and 60.9 KB after; 308 to 353 microseconds before, 72 microseconds after. The start of pig does not get measurably shorter: 30 warm starts in json mode with a Node extension had a median first reply of 311.8 ms before and 316.5 ms after, and 16.5 ms before and 16.6 ms after without an extension, both within the noise of that host.
