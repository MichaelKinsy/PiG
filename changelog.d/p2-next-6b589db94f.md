### Added

- `ai.GetPromptCacheTtlMs`, `ai.GetCacheWarmingDelayMs`, `ai.IsCacheWarmingReplayable`, `ai.GetCacheWarmingRequestOptions`, `ai.EvaluateCacheWarming`, `ai.CacheWarmingDecision` and the `ai.CacheWarming*` constants: the prompt cache warming policy shared by every host, moved out of the coding agent's cache warmer (Pi c9552c7d, #11857). The coding agent's `GetPromptCacheTtlMs`, `GetCacheWarmingDelayMs` and `IsReplayable` are gone from `internal/codingagent`; `CacheWarmingAction` and `CacheWarmingDecision` there and in `coding/extension` are the `ai` types. <!-- feat: p2-next-cache-warming -->
- p2's `cache-warming` plugin and the `cacheWarming` setting (`"idle"` by default, `"streaming"` or `"off"`): before the prompt cache entry of a conversation's last request expires, p2 sends the request again with a one-token answer while that is cheaper than writing the cache again, and counts the refreshes in the conversation's usage. `HarnessParts.RequestOf` hands a Durable `afterResponse` hook the request that produced its message, and the worker's liveness has a `cacheWarming` count. <!-- feat: p2-next-cache-warming -->
