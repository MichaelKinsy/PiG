### Changed

- CI: `make module-publication` gives `go mod download` of a nested module 600 seconds instead of 60 (`--download-timeout SECONDS`), because the public module proxy answers a new pseudo-version with a timeout for up to half an hour while it fetches the repository and the direct fallback needs a minute or more under load. A download that still does not finish fails with a message that names the proxy delay and the `.info` URL to poll, instead of a bare timeout.
