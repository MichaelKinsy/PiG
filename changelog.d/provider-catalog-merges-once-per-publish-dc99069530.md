### Fixed

- With a large fetched model catalog, every model lookup through `CreateProvider` re-merged the provider's baseline and fetched catalogs on every read, slowing model selection and request setup; the catalog is now merged once when published (stored restore or network refresh) and cached, with public getters returning an owned copy and internal lookups reading the cached snapshot directly.
