### Fixed

- A Chord object whose factory reads a key of another object (`at(key)`) could fail to go live with `not_ready` ("app.exposer: not live yet") when that object failed and went live again, in memory, while the key's first pin still loaded from the store: the key was handed back before its pin on the new instance had landed, and the factory's derive read it with no value. In Pi a new pin whose load has finished when the old pin settles has landed by then; here the key is now handed back after the new pin has settled when its load has finished. A new pin whose load has not finished when the old pin settles is not waited for, as in Pi, where the consumer's get is then rejected with `not_ready` and `activation_failed`. In the few steps between a load's end and the key being marked loaded the two can differ either way: PiG fails where Pi succeeds, or succeeds where Pi fails.
