### Changed

- RPC and JSON mode write each event with less CPU and fewer goroutines. The output tail keeps one writer goroutine instead of starting one for every burst of records (200 turns of the arena RPC workload: 8,251 goroutines created before, 2,642 after), and the hot records (`message_start`, `message_update`, `message_end`, `turn_*`, `agent_*`, `tool_execution_*`) are encoded straight into the output line without the reflection walk: encoding a `message_end` takes 3.0 µs and 33 allocations instead of 18.1 µs and 81, a `turn_end` 3.4 µs instead of 21.6 µs. Per-turn CPU of `--mode rpc` in the arena harness (linux/arm64, interleaved runs against the release before this change, with its GC target): 2.01 to 1.59 ms at 20 turns (median of 7, A/A medians 0.02 ms apart) and 3.93 to 3.58 ms at 200 turns (median of 14, A/A medians 0.04 ms apart), with the same peak RSS. The bytes of every record, their order and the output-failure and termination behaviour are unchanged.
