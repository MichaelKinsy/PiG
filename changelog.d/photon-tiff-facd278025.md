### Fixed

- A TIFF image is now decoded the way Pi decodes it (Photon links the `tiff` 0.9.1 crate under `image` 0.24.9). A TIFF on the X11 or Wayland clipboard (`image/tiff`), an image the model is sent under any declared type, and a TIFF handed to the terminal image conversion or to the durable `read` tool are converted to PNG or resized exactly as Pi does, byte for byte, where PiG used to answer that it could not convert them. Accepted: 8- and 16-bit gray (BlackIsZero and WhiteIsZero), RGB, RGBA and 8-bit CMYK, strips and tiles, chunky or planar, big- or little-endian, uncompressed, LZW, PackBits, Deflate and JPEG, with or without the horizontal predictor. Refused, as in Pi: palette, bilevel, 1- to 7-bit, 12-bit, 32-bit and floating point images, YCbCr, the floating point predictor, Fax and old-style JPEG, BigTIFF, damaged files, and images of more than 512 MiB decoded. <!-- fix: facd278025 -->
