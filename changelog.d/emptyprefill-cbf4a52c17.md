### Fixed

- The Go and Rust SDKs send an empty `prefill` of `Editor`/`editor` and an empty `placeholder` of `Input`/`input` as given, as Pi's `ctx.ui.editor(title, "")` and `ctx.ui.input(title, "")` do (an RPC client sees `prefill: ""`). They used to drop it, so a port of Pi's plan-mode example wrote a different RPC request than Pi. Pi's `editor(title)` and `input(title)`, which write no such member, are now `EditorWithoutPrefill`, `InputWithoutPlaceholder` and `InputWithoutPlaceholderWithOptions` in Go and `editor_without_prefill`, `input_without_placeholder` and `input_without_placeholder_with_options` in Rust, as `ClearStatus` is for `SetStatus`. `TestConformance_UIEmptyMembersAgreeAcrossSDKs` now requires every SDK (isolated, packed, fused) to tell the empty string from the unset member. <!-- fix: cbf4a52c17 -->
