### Fixed

- A closed Chord workers component forks no worker. A start whose app `start` data had arrived looked at `Close` in one step and forked in the next, so a `Close` that landed between the two spawned a worker process into the closed component: it showed as `starting` in the workers' state, the start's own cleanup killed it before `Close` returned, and the idle timer `Close` had stopped was armed again. The look and the fork are one step now, as in Pi, and a start that meets a closed component fails `released/closed` without forking.
