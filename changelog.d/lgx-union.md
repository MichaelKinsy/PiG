### Changed

- `SessionEntry` (`coding.SessionEntry`, `extension.SessionEntry`) is Pi's `SessionEntry` union, an interface whose members are the entry types of `session-manager.ts`: message, thinking level change, model change, usage, compaction, branch summary, custom, custom message, context edit, label and session info entries. Read an entry's `type`, `id`, `parentId` and `timestamp` with `entry.Base()` (they were the `Base` field), and its JSON with `entry.Raw()`. An entry keeps its JSON as it was read, so members this build does not know and the member order survive loading and rewriting a session file, as in Pi; a record that no member describes (a newer entry type, or a known type with ill-typed members) is a `RawEntry`. The session file's `FileEntry` is the entries and `SessionHeader`.
