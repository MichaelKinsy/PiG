### Fixed

- `/copy`, the copy key, RPC `get_last_assistant_text` and a child session's `getLastAssistantText` now skip a turn that ended without an answer. After Esc during a tool call or after a provider error they copy or return the previous answer; before, `/copy` said `No agent messages to copy yet.` and RPC returned `null` while the answer was on screen. An assistant message without text is skipped when its turn did not end normally (aborted, failed, only tool calls, or cut at the length limit); a reply that ended normally without text is still the last reply.
