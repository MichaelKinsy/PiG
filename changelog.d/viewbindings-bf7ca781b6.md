### Added

- An extension binds keys and gestures to the intents of a view it mounts: `bindings [{key | gesture, intent, arg?, name?}]` on `ui.view.mount` and `ui.view.update`, in the Go, Node, Python, Rust and Lisp SDKs (at most 32). A key is a key id as `registerShortcut` writes it (`d`, `shift+left`, `ctrl+shift+p`); a gesture is `swipe-left`, `swipe-right`, `swipe-up` or `swipe-down`. A binding raises an intent that names no node, with a constant argument, and several bindings may raise one intent with different arguments. A binding is never global: a client fires it only while the mount holds the focus and the focused node did not take the key. The host refuses a key bound twice and the reserved keys (`escape`, `tab`, `f6`, and `ctrl` with `c`, `d` or `z`) with `invalid`, and sends every client the bindings as part of the content its digest covers. A client that cannot honour a binding reports it, and the extension reads the notice with `ui.view.diagnostics`. The reference web client and the terminal client do not fire bindings yet. One conformance row, `TestConformance_UIViewBindings`, runs in every SDK and realization.
