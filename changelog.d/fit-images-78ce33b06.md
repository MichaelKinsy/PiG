### Added

- `durable/images/fit`, as Pi's `@earendil-works/pi-durable/images/fit`, fits the images entering a conversation to its model's limits with any `tools.ImageProcessor`, before they are stored, as the coding agent does: `fit.CreateFitImagesExtension(processor)` is an `afterTool` hook for the results of every tool the model calls (MCP tools, code mode's `image()`), and `fit.FitImage(ctx, bytes, mimeType, limits, processor)` and `fit.FitImages(ctx, content, limits, processor)` fit a host's input before it is submitted. An image that cannot be prepared is left out with a note. `tools.ImageLimitsOf(model)` (Pi's `imageLimits`) resolves a model's limits as `read` does, and `tools.PreparedNotes`, `tools.FromBase64` and `tools.DetectSupportedImageMimeType` are exported.
