### Fixed

- An extension's overlay (`ctx.ui.custom` with `overlay: true`) that keeps colored rows until its `invalidate()` is called kept the old theme after a theme change, in the regular and the fullscreen screen, and after a theme change in `/settings` it was not drawn again at all, where Pi redraws it. PiG's `Invalidate` set a flag on the root of the screen and reached no component, where Pi's `TUI.invalidate` calls `invalidate()` on every mounted root and every overlay's component. It now reaches both, the host tells a subprocess extension's component (`ui.custom.invalidate`, after the new palette), the Node runtime calls its `invalidate()`, and a theme set invalidates twice as Pi's does. Extensions on the Go, Python, Rust and Lisp runtimes ignore the notify until those runtimes handle it. <!-- fix: 98d708f331, 2e82be6163, 557b2210a1 -->
