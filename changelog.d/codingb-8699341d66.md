### Added

- The read tool's operations take Pi's `createReadStream` in every SDK: `ReadOperations.CreateReadStream` in Go (`sdk` and `internal/codingagent/tools`), `create_read_stream` in Python, `create_read_stream` and `ReadStream` in Rust and `:create-read-stream` in Lisp. A tool that supplies it reads a text file through the stream and keeps only the lines it shows in memory, where `readFile` loads the whole file. The host reads the stream in chunks of at most 64 KB and closes it when the read ends or is aborted.
