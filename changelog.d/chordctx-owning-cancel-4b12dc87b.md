### Fixed

- Chord: the cancel function `chordctx.WithCancel` returns now aborts the context and runs its abort listeners (`OnAbort`) only in the call that cancels it first. Before, a repeated call (a `defer cancel()` after an explicit one, a timer and an owner) that arrived after the first call had cancelled the context and before it had taken the listeners took them away and ran them on its own goroutine, outside the section that cancelled: a waiter's abort listener enqueues its continuation where it runs, so a loop section that resolved a promise and then cancelled its context had the abort delivered behind the settlement's reaction, and the waiter delivered the result instead of the abort. A call after the first now records no cause and takes no listener, as Pi's `abort()` of an aborted signal does nothing; it returns once the context is cancelled, while the listeners are the first call's to run (they may still be running when it returns).
