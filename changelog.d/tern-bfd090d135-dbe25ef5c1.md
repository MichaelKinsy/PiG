### Fixed

- Starting a child host could wait out its 30-second ready timeout ("child not ready in time") although the child was up. The Go port of Node's IPC channel read messages in its own goroutine and dropped any that arrived before its owner had registered a listener. Node cannot lose them, because an owner registers in the tick that creates the channel. A child that opened its channel after the parent's init message had been written could miss the init (so it never answered), and a manager descheduled between spawning the child and registering its control listener could miss the child's ready. The channel now keeps the messages received before its first listener and replays them to it in order, and a forked child instance does the same for its control listeners. <!-- fix: dbe25ef5c1 -->
