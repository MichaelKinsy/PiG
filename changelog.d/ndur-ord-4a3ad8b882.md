### Fixed

- The Session orders the continuations of what a publication resolved before the next part of the same storage call is published at any number of goroutines in the process. The ordering used to read a dump of every goroutine, cost about a millisecond per publication, and was given up above 128 goroutines. The waits of the Session (`SessionImpl.Park`, `SessionImpl.Await`, a commit caller's resumption) now register before they block and acknowledge when they resumed, so a publication costs microseconds and 4 KiB, whatever the process runs. A caller that needs its continuation ordered up to its next wait of the Session passes `session.WithContinuation(ctx)` and calls `Entered`. `SessionImpl.ReadOnLineCtx` and `ReadWithTicketCtx` run a line read with the caller's line admission and continuation. <!-- fix: 4a3ad8b882 -->
