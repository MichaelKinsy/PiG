### Fixed

- A running watch drain registers the call it makes next when its frame is published, so `AwaitEntered` and the Session's hop between two parts of a call see every listener call that is not yet entered; frames published behind a running call no longer fold into one call at more than one processor. <!-- fix: 68a31878e8 -->
- The Session's blocking points a listener can reach (the wait for the previous line job, for room, for the writer's drain and for a part's publication) mark the delivery entered, so a listener that calls `UnloadDocuments` during a multi-part call does not stall the Session's publications. <!-- fix: 674b44978e -->

### Changed

- A watch listener that blocks on something the host cannot see (a channel, a lock, network I/O, a child process) calls `chord.DeliveryEntered()` first, or blocks on its own goroutine; until then it holds back the Harness waits and the Session's publications that follow its delivery (divergence D224).
