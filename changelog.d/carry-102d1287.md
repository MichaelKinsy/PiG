### Fixed

- The `env` daemon reports a watch `change` whose path list would not fit in a frame (16 MiB less 64 KiB) as an overflow event, which clients read as "rescan". An oversized list used to make the frame writer fail and end the daemon's only output writer, leaving every request hanging until the client's 30 s silence limit.
