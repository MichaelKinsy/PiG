### Fixed

- `WaitForTask` on a task whose terminal commit was adopted no longer fails with "Harness is closed" when a close begins before the wait wakes: a host `SubscribeCommits` listener that closes the Harness on that commit, or a close that comes between the commit's adoption and its publication. Pi settles the wait at the commit, so a later close cannot reach it; PiG settled it only after the host listeners, and the close's seal rejected it, turning a completed task into an error for its waiter. The receipt is now fixed at the commit and the wait only wakes after the listeners. <!-- fix: 9a9dc9900a4754a7e959b42d9bee188fbcc4500d -->
- `WaitForIdle` on the Harness or a Conversation no longer resumes before the `SubscribeCommits` listeners saw the commit that made the work idle. The idle settle that ends a reconcile pass, and the one the context expiry timer runs, could decide on a commit another goroutine had just adopted and resolve the waits ahead of its host listeners; Pi's continuation there cannot run between an adoption and its publication, and PiG's now leaves the decision to the settle queued behind the listeners. <!-- fix: e65ade9b1b81693d3d880ad62a0ed074423cb395 -->
