### Fixed

- Markdown rendering matches Pi for more block shapes. A quote whose list takes lazy lines no longer swallows the quoted line after them, and an empty nested quote drops the lines after it as Pi does. Display math that starts a line after a paragraph line (up to three spaces in) clips the paragraph like Pi, and a paragraph ended by a pipe line and a dash line merges with the next one when display math follows later in the text. Nested lists followed by blank lines or trailing spaces inside a list item keep their empty row, a list item's text starts a setext heading at any line, lazy lines of a quote are lexed apart from the quoted lines before them (HTML blocks, tables, display math and setext headings no longer read across them), and an unclosed fence keeps a partial closing fence line when its text ends in a newline. <!-- fix: 462d5c95b6 -->
