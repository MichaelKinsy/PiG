### Fixed

- Chord: a context linked from two abort sources now reports the reason of the source that aborted first when one of the sources is derived from a `chordctx.WithoutAbortSignal` context: the host's linked abort signals do, and so does `chordctx.OnAbort` on a context derived by `WithAbortSignal`. Before, the abort order (`chordctx.AbortOrder`) walked through the `WithoutAbortSignal` context into its parents, so the abort of a parent that never reached the derived context counted as the derived context's own, and its reason won over the other source's although that one aborted first. As in Pi, where `withoutAbortSignal` leaves the context no abort signal, the walk now ends at a `WithoutAbortSignal` context. Released in: v0.7.0, and every release since (the walk came with 0b76ae5e5, which `git tag --contains` places in v0.7.0; a probe at the v0.7.0 tag ranks `WithCancel(WithoutAbortSignal(x))` before an independent source that aborted earlier). <!-- fix: 1a89bc25f --> <!-- cause: abortSources and AbortOrder each walked a context's parents on their own, and only abortSources stopped at a WithoutAbortSignal context, so the two functions disagreed about which aborts reach a context. -->
