### Fixed

- An image the model receives is now JPEG-encoded with a port of Photon's encoder (image 0.24.9: the IJG integer DCT, the standard Huffman tables, the libjpeg quality scaling) instead of Go's `image/jpeg`, so for an image that decodes to the pixels Photon decodes (PNG, GIF, BMP) the bytes and sizes are Pi's, and with them every decision that depends on a size: which quality of the ladder fits the byte limit, how far the 3/4 shrink goes, and which of the PNG and the JPEG candidate of a resize is smaller (Pi #11577). The encoder is 1.9 times faster than Go's on a screenshot and 2.4 times faster on a 12 MP photo, with a dozen allocations in place of millions. D165 now covers only the JPEG and WebP decoders, whose pixels are still Go's. <!-- fix: 95de1de195 -->
