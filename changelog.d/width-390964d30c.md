### Fixed

- A program that embeds the alternate-screen renderer without a `CopySelection` function no longer copies a selection holding a lone UTF-16 surrogate as invalid UTF-8 through its OSC 52 fallback. PiG base64-encoded the three internal bytes of the unit, where Pi encodes it as U+FFFD (`Buffer.from(text)`); the `pig` command always installs `CopySelection`, so only embedders saw it. <!-- fix: 390964d30ccfcd21401ce3c415f7fac20af549df -->
