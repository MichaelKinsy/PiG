### Changed

- `cacheWarming` now defaults to `"idle"` instead of `"streaming"`, as in Pi: PiG also keeps eligible prompt caches warm for up to 30 minutes between agent runs, but only when a refresh is expected to pay for itself. Set `"cacheWarming": "streaming"` in the global settings for the previous behavior.
- The `/login` and `/logout` provider lists and the `/login <provider>` completions now show the preferred partners Radius and OpenRouter first, then all other providers alphabetically. In the provider lists, partners are marked with `✦`, the selected Radius row shimmers in the Radius logo colors and the selected OpenRouter row glows in OpenRouter's brand color (lime on dark themes, violet on light themes).
