### Fixed

- The Chord terminal launcher no longer ends when the sandbox closes a link socket while it is still connecting and then sends on it (`send on closed channel`): every state change and close of a link socket runs on the launcher's loop, a dial that finishes after the close is closed at once, and a send on a socket that is not open is dropped, as in Pi. <!-- fix: d8c67842e9 -->
- A terminal client whose sandbox stops reading its input and floods messages can be stopped again (Ctrl-C, `Stop`): the sandbox's end no longer waits on the launcher's full event queue from the loop itself. The terminal is no longer left in raw mode when the sandbox ends while the launcher is starting. <!-- fix: d8c67842e9 -->
- The terminal's frame and bundle limits count bytes, as Pi's `Buffer.byteLength` checks do, again. <!-- fix: 45794934c0 -->
