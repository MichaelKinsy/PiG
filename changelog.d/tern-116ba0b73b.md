### Changed

- A turn on the Anthropic Messages API spends less CPU building its request. The request was copied and walked by reflection on every turn, looking for large strings to cache, even in a history that holds none, before it was encoded; the encoder now takes those strings itself without the walk, and the body and the `anthropic-beta` header are the same. In an RPC session of one tool call and reply per turn (linux/arm64), the median turn CPU is 4.39 ms at 200 turns, against 4.73 ms before (20 turns: 2.12 ms against 2.21 ms; 1000 turns: 5.32 ms against 5.46 ms, within run-to-run spread).
