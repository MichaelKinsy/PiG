### Added

- The Go, Rust, Python and Lisp SDKs serve TypeBox's `Value.Check` and `Value.Errors` (Go `sdk.Value.Check` and `Errors`, Rust `pig_sdk::value::Value::check` and `errors`, Python `pig_sdk.Value.check` and `errors`, Lisp `value-check` and `value-errors`), so an extension tool can check the constraints of its `Type.Refine` refinements in `execute()`, where Pi 1.5.2 asks authors to check them, as a Node extension does with `Value` from `typebox/value`. The refinements run in the extension, in TypeBox's order (a node's refinements after its other keywords; `Check` stops at the first failure, `Errors` words each failure with the refinement's message), checked against typebox 1.3.27 itself. Tool arguments are still not refined, as in Pi. Only `Check` and `Errors` are served, not the other members of `Value` (D226).
