### Fixed

- chord: a client whose link dropped while it was away (a laptop or phone woken, a network changed) reconnects at once instead of first waiting for its `CanRetry` check: with a check that takes 3 s, the next attempt went 3 250 ms after the wake and now goes at 0 ms. The wait happened because the connection loop scheduled its next attempt only once `CanRetry` had answered. `KeepConnected` now asks `CanRetry` beside the next attempt, one question at a time (a link that drops while a question is open asks nothing more); a late `false` still stops the loop and ends the attempt made meanwhile, which is reported `waiting`; a drop within 26 s of a wake is retried at once, once per wake, and the replacement of a silent socket no longer waits a pause. `StartClient` stops the loop before it calls `Outdated`, so the app starts over with the link already ended. The vendored page and plugin runtimes carry the same change (Pi 21e4cb80d: `surfaces/connection.ts`, `surfaces/client.ts`, `web/page.ts`). The old loop is in v0.2.0, v0.3.0 and v0.7.0. <!-- fix: acc14924dfa886d3ce2b1914293cc757906cce54, 6e061374e278c20fadb251fd4cbae1b613e3fa60 -->
