### Fixed

- An RPC `set_session_name` or `set_thinking_level` no longer waits for an extension's `session_info_changed` or `thinking_level_select` handler written with the Go, Rust, Python or Lisp SDK: Pi replies at once because it does not await that event, but PiG's reply was held until the handler returned, for ever when the handler waited on a socket or a timer and never came back, and now waits at most the 250 ms that already bounds the other handlers a runtime cannot report waiting. A handler that computes for longer than that writes its own notification after the reply, where Pi writes it before; the handler's effect is not lost.
