## D393 Calls in flight survive a hold's loading unbind

What: PiG ports Pi main 6515a7977 (`feat(chord): calls in flight survive a loading unbind`), which is not in Pi 1.5.0. A hold unbinding with `loading` (a restart, a parked swap, a live failure retrying) keeps its calls in flight, and each gets one answer from what it waits on (the instance it runs on, or the object's gate). Calls sent by the old method names before the consumer learns of the `loading` wait at the gate too. `failed` and removal still reject. Pi 1.5.0 rejected them `unavailable` ("its object rebinds"). Observable difference from Pi: none known.

Why: A restart or a parked swap rejected the calls in flight.

Pi bug: Calls in flight on a hold are rejected when its object restarts or parks (1.5.0).

Scope: `chord/next/host/remote_hold.go` (`unbind`, `names`, `pending`) and `remote_provider.go`.
