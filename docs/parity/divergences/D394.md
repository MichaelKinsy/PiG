## D394 A call crossing a failed, a removal or a fresh bound is answered by what its consumer knows

What: PiG ports the two provider-side reload fixes of Pi main edc9c6989 (`feat(chord): the reload suite, kit timeouts and base marks, four reload fixes`), which is not in Pi 1.5.0. A call sent before its consumer learnt of a `failed` or a removal is answered with the cause the consumer was told, never `not_ready`; a call sent with the indexes of a binding whose newer `bound` its consumer has not acknowledged is decoded by that binding's names (one the new variant lacks fails `invalid/member`). The page-side fixes of that commit (script fetch retry, serial `applyChanges`) belong to the surfaces port. Observable difference from Pi: none known.

Why: A call that crossed a failure was answered `not_ready`, and a call by an old variant's index ran another variant's method.

Pi bug: Calls crossing a `failed` get `not_ready`; calls by a stale variant's indexes are decoded by the new names (1.5.0).

Scope: `chord/next/host/remote_hold.go` (`refusal`, `names`, `bindTo`) and `remote_provider.go` (`acknowledged`).
