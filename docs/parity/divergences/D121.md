## D121 The Anthropic SSE reader holds back a carriage return that ends a chunk (pi-mono #10390)

What: Pi's `consumeLine` (`anthropic-messages.ts:454-466`) ends a line at a `\r` that is the last character of a network chunk. The `\n` of a CRLF split that way then arrives as an empty line, flushes the record without its `data`, and the turn aborts with `Could not parse Anthropic SSE event message_start: Unexpected end of JSON input; data=`. PiG waits for the next chunk before it ends a line at a trailing `\r`; at the end of the stream the `\r` ends its line as before.

Why: where a proxy flushes CR and LF separately, every turn through it fails. A CRLF is one line break in the SSE standard.

Scope: `anthropicSSEReader` only (Anthropic Messages and the providers that use it). The OpenAI-compatible decoder already holds the `\r` back. Chunks without a split CRLF decode exactly as Pi does.

Owner decision: 2026-10-10, owner ruling "fix every confirmed bug now, ported from Pi or not".

Call-site markers: `ai/anthropic_stream.go` (`anthropicConsumeLine`).

Evidence: `TestAnthropicStreamSurvivesCRLFSplitAcrossChunks` (`ai/anthropic_sse_crlf_split_test.go`, whole provider path, every CR and LF in its own chunk) and the `carriage return at a chunk end` rows of `TestAnthropicSSEReaderLineDecoding`; removing the hold-back fails them.

Parity allowance: Pi 1.4.0 aborts on this input (reproduced on the previous pin with a mock that flushes `\r` and `\n` separately; the Anthropic SSE reader is unchanged in Pi 1.4.0), so no paired scenario asserts it.

Remove when: Pi holds back a trailing `\r` in `consumeLine`.
