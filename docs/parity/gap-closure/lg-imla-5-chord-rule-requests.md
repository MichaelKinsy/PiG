## Addendum: U5 after the discriminators became named types

`ServiceProviderUpdate` and `WireServiceProviderUpdate` now pass the U5 discriminator check (`ServiceProviderUpdateType`, six typed constants). The detector's next U5 finding is "the Go struct has no field for the property `instance` of a union member". Pi has one `instance` property with two shapes: an address (`state`, `closed`) and an instance snapshot (`spawned`). The tagged Go struct carries them as `Address *ServiceInstanceAddress` (state/closed) and `Snapshot *ServiceInstanceSnapshot` (spawned, serialized under `"instance"`), because one Go field cannot hold both types. Request for lg-rules: let U5 accept a tagged-struct member property that maps to several typed fields when the wire codec writes each under the upstream key (`MarshalJSON` in `internal/chord/state_wire.go:74-98`, `wire.go:128-205`), or record the rename `instance -> Address|Snapshot` as reviewed. The same applies to the `agent` `ProxyAssistantMessageEvent` rows once the detector reaches them.

## Addendum: delta codec factories are missing from the public chord/delta (corrects an earlier draft of this note)

`pkg:chord/delta#encoder` and `#decoder` (`delta/index.ts:523` and its decoder twin), together with `WireOp` and `assertValidWireOp`, exist only in `internal/chord/delta` (`codec.go`: `NewEncoder`, `NewDecoder`, `Encoder.Encode`, `Decoder.Decode`; `delta.go`: `WireOp`, `AssertValidWireOp`). The public `chord/delta` package has the tracker, `Op`, `IsReplace`, `IsBase`, `AssertValidOp` and `ApplyImmutable`, but no wire codec, although Pi publishes `encoder`/`decoder`/`WireOp` from `@earendil-works/chord/delta`. That is a real public-surface gap, not a rename: closing it needs `Encoder`, `Decoder`, `WireOp` and `AssertValidWireOp` in `chord/delta` over its own `Op = []any`, and the ported cases of `packages/chord/test/delta.test.ts` (encoder/decoder, wire grammar). The two `delta` packages are separate implementations (public: `Op = []any` with `invalidOp` errors; internal: a distinct `Op` with `Verb()`), so a type alias cannot share the codec. Resolved: the codec is ported into the public `chord/delta` (`chord/delta/codec.go`: `WireOp`, `AssertValidWireOp`, `Encoder`/`NewEncoder`, `Decoder`/`NewDecoder`) over its own `Op = []any` with `invalidOp`/`PathError`/`UnsafePathError` errors, following the existing split where the public package carries its own tracker; `chord/delta/codec_upstream_test.go` ports the `delta codec` cases and the wire-grammar assertions (mutation: interning on first use fails 9 subtests).
